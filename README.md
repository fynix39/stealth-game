Silent Steps — a midnight museum heist. Sneak through one top-down level, stay out of the guards' vision cones, hide behind crates and in shadows, grab three gems, and reach the green exit unseen. Guards walk fixed patrol routes you can learn; suspicion fills inside a cone and drains when you slip away; a full meter raises the alarm and the guards hunt you — get caught three times and the night is over.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, hold Shift to sneak, Space to start.
